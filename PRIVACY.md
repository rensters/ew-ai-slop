# privacy policy for ew AI slop, nty

short version: it collects nothing. like, actually nothing.

- **no data collection.** no names, emails, history, clicks, what you watch, none of it.
- **no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** there's no server. there's nothing to send stuff to.
- **your settings stay in your browser.** the on/off switches are saved in your browser's extension storage (and a copy in youtube's own local storage so the page doesn't flash AI junk before they load). they never leave your computer, except through your browser's own sync if you have that on.
- **the one network request:** if you turn on the opt-in "real titles everywhere" switch, it asks youtube itself (youtube.com/oembed) for the original titles of the videos you're looking at. that request goes to youtube and nowhere else, and it's off by default.
- **"copy debug info"** just copies a report to your clipboard. it only goes anywhere if you paste it somewhere yourself.

it only runs on www.youtube.com, nowhere else.
